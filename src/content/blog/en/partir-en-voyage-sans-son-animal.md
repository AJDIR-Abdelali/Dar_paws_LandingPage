A weekend in Agadir, a wedding in Fès, a family holiday… and the same question comes back: **who will look after the dog or the cat?** Here's a simple checklist so you can leave with peace of mind.

## 1. Start early

During holidays and festivities, good pet sitters get booked quickly. Start looking **one to two weeks before** you leave, especially for several nights.

## 2. Check the health record

Before you leave your pet with someone:

- their **vaccinations are up to date**, especially rabies;
- their **flea, tick and worm** treatment is recent;
- you've written down their **medication** and doses.

> Tip: in DarPaws, add vaccinations and vet visits to your pet's profile. The app shows what's coming up next, and you can find everything in a second.

## 3. Choose the right kind of care

- **Home visits**: ideal for cats, who prefer to stay in their own home.
- **Walks**: for a dog that's alone a few hours a day.
- **Sitting or boarding**: your pet stays with the sitter while you're away.

## 4. Choose a pet sitter you trust

Look at their profile, **services**, **rate**, **distance** and above all the **reviews** from other owners. On DarPaws, only owners who completed a booking can leave a review.

Don't hesitate to **meet before you leave**: your pet gets to know them, and you can ask all your questions.

## 5. Prepare an instruction sheet

Leave your pet sitter:

- **meal** times and amounts;
- habits (walks, games, favourite hiding spots);
- your **vet's** number and an emergency contact;
- what **not** to do (forbidden foods, doors to keep closed…).

## 6. Keep the same routine

Leave their usual food, bed, blanket or a piece of clothing that smells like you. Fewer changes mean less stress.

## 7. Stay in touch… in moderation

One photo a day is enough to reassure you. Trust your pet sitter and enjoy your trip!
